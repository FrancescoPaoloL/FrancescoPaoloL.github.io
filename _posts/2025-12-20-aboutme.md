## About Me (updated 10-2026)
I'm a Senior Backend Engineer working on AI security. My background is .NET/Azure production systems, monitoring, and incident response.

AI is going to be part of the systems we build, whether we are ready for it or not. I'm interested in understanding how these systems actually work, where their trust boundaries are, and what can go wrong before we start relying on them in practice.

I build tools that make the implicit trust model in LLM systems explicit. I come to AI security from C, llama.cpp and model internals, plus application security applied to LLMs and agents.

My open-source projects:

* [llmSecurityDemo](https://github.com/FrancescoPaoloL/llmSecurityDemo): detects vulnerabilities from the OWASP LLM Top 10.
* [llm-tollgate-poc](https://github.com/FrancescoPaoloL/llm-tollgate-poc): a gateway between agents and their tools, built around the difference between trust, authorization and containment.
* [imgpoison](https://github.com/FrancescoPaoloL/imgpoison): steganography for adversarial input.
* [lockfile-analyzer](https://github.com/FrancescoPaoloL/lockfile-analyzer): supply-chain analysis for npm lockfiles.
* [xsa_POC](https://github.com/FrancescoPaoloL/xsa_POC): a replication study on GPT-2 internals.


I also write about AI, security, and the assumptions we make around these systems here. The writing is not always technical; it is often where I explore a question before turning it into an engineering problem.
